About TIS

Built for young people who refuse to think small.

The Intellectual Summit exists so that a young person's postcode does not decide the size of their ambition.

The platform

One local room. A global standard.

The Intellectual Summit trains young people to think clearly, speak with conviction, and lead beyond their immediate circle.

We bring structured mentorship, public speaking practice, and honest conversation about purpose to a generation ready to lead, not just observe.

What began as a single webinar has grown into a full platform: talks, workshops, and a yearly gathering that draws students, young professionals, and mentors into one room to think together.

Founder's note

Relevance is not given. It is built, one clear idea and one honest room at a time.

Dr Jochebed Emuveyan

Founder, The Intellectual Summit

What we do

Three ways the platform shows up.

The same standard runs through everything TIS puts in a room.

Talks

Sessions built around ideas worth defending, delivered to a room of students, young professionals and mentors rather than to a passive audience.

Workshops

Practical work on speaking, structure and direction. Participants do the work in the session rather than take notes about it.

The yearly gathering

A single day that brings the whole platform into one hall. In 2026 that day is 7 November, at Okunozee Hall in Okada.

Our approach

Structured, honest, and built to leave something behind.

  • Structure over motivation. Sessions are designed around outcomes, not applause.
  • Practice over theory. Speaking is trained in front of a real room.
  • Honesty over comfort. Feedback is direct because it has to be useful.
  • Mixed rooms. Students, young professionals and mentors think together.
  • A global benchmark. The standard in the room is not the local average.
  • Continuity. One webinar became a platform; the yearly gathering keeps it moving.
Who is in the room

Students, young professionals, mentors.

The Summit draws all three into one hall on purpose. Ambition grows fastest where it is measured against people slightly ahead of you.

Meet the founder

Dr Jochebed Emuveyan.

Scholar, leadership coach, and the founder of The Intellectual Summit — building a generation of purpose driven young leaders under the mantra "Global Relevance."

PhD, International Relations Scholar & Leadership Coach Lecturer, Igbinedion University

Dr. Jochebed Emuveyan is a scholar, leadership coach, and the founder of The Intellectual Summit (TIS), a platform dedicated to raising a generation of purpose driven young leaders under the mantra "Global Relevance." She holds a PhD in International Relations from Igbinedion University, Nigeria, where she also lectures and mentors students on academic and personal growth.

Beyond the classroom, Dr. Emuveyan is passionate about international diplomacy and global affairs, and she channels that passion into building young people who think critically, lead confidently, and live with purpose. Through TIS, she creates spaces, from webinars to in person events, where students and young professionals are equipped with the mindset and tools to navigate their journey with clarity and conviction.

Grounded in faith and driven by a deep belief in human potential, she continues to build platforms and communities that inspire the next generation to see themselves as globally relevant.
