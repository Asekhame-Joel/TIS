The courage to speak

Practical public speaking and communication training, tested in front of a real room, not a mirror.

What it is

Voice: why it exists

Voice is the training track. Clear thinking that never leaves your head changes nothing, so TIS treats speaking as a skill to be practised under pressure rather than a talent you either have or do not.

Workshops cover structure, delivery and the discipline of saying less. Participants speak in front of an actual audience of peers and mentors and receive direct, usable feedback in the same session.

The emphasis is on conviction rather than performance. The goal is a young person who can hold a room, defend an idea, and stay clear when questioned.

What you get

  • Build and deliver an argument that holds together
  • Speak in front of a live room, not a rehearsal mirror
  • Receive honest feedback from peers and mentors
  • Handle questions and pushback without losing the thread
