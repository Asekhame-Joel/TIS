Why choose us

Reasons to spend a day in this room.

TIS is built around outcomes, honest conversation and a standard that is deliberately wider than the local average.

Outcome led, not motivation led

Every talk, workshop and mentorship track is built around three outcomes: purpose, voice and reach. Sessions are designed to leave something behind, not just a good feeling.

One room, mixed generations

Students, young professionals and mentors sit in the same room. The conversation is not filtered through a stage; it happens between people who need each other.

Practice in front of a real audience

Public speaking is trained where it counts, in front of a live room, with feedback that is honest enough to be useful.

A global standard from a local room

TIS is a Lagos born platform with a deliberate global benchmark. One local room, a global standard.

Honest conversation

The founding position is plain: relevance is not given, it is built, one clear idea and one honest room at a time. That sets the tone of every session.

A platform, not a one off

What began as a single webinar has grown into talks, workshops and a yearly gathering. Attending the Summit connects you to the wider platform.

Benefits of attending

What you take home from the day.

  • A definition of success you chose rather than inherited
  • A direction clear enough to write down
  • Public speaking practice tested in front of a live room
  • Direct feedback from peers and mentors
  • Conversations with people working to a wider standard
  • A place inside a platform that keeps meeting, not a one off ticket
